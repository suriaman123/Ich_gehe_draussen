I guess I'm still a little tired from the GO! event. I woke up in the morning, had breakfast, and then went straight back to sleep. Eventually, I woke up again at around 14:40.

I called Mom since she hadn't called me yet. While talking to her, I made myself a shake, drank it, and headed out just in time to catch the bus. (Also, my room is still a mess)

I reached Cosmos, and the chef was there today. While I was standing awkwardly near the counter, I heard someone call my name. I turned around and saw Prince. He had come to collect the decorations from the Diwali party. Prince was eating some kheer, but he generously offered it to me, and I happily accepted.

A minute later, Utkarsh arrived as well.

Today was the day for cooking with the elderly residents, and I could see them around the place. After finishing my kheer, I went into the kitchen to see what was happening. I asked Anna for an update, and apparently, today's menu was lasagna. Well, when I say we were making lasagna, I mean other people were making lasagna. The lady who speaks English was busy preparing the sauce, while three elderly residents were preparing the salad. As for me, I spent most of the time hanging out with Utkarsh and Prince.

Ketan and another girl were in the side room, but they seemed busy among themselves, so after greeting them, I decided not to disturb them.

At some point, Prince and Utkarsh planned to leave. However, I stopped them and insisted that they should at least stay and eat the lasagna. After all, if we're already here, we might as well enjoy the food. Thankfully, they agreed to stay.

Soon, it was time for the meal.

Now, technically, I was already full. I had drunk my shake at around 15:00 and then eaten a bowl of kheer when I arrived. But I suppose there is a limit to how much self-control one can expect from a person when free food is involved.

And the lasagna was damn good!

It was a slightly downgraded version of the one we had made a couple of weeks ago, but it still tasted great. I also had a small serving of salad to maintain the illusion of a balanced diet.

The meal was over surprisingly quickly. I said goodbye to everyone and caught the 17:57 bus back.

On the way home, I decided to stop at Aldi. However, as soon as I entered the store, I realised that the only thing I actually needed was bread. Did that stop me from shopping? Of course not. I wandered around the store and somehow ended up buying around €20 worth of stuff. A very successful trip, considering I had only gone there for bread.

There wasn't another bus for the next 30 minutes, so I decided to walk home. I didn't have that much stuff with me anyway, and I reached home before 19:00.

And that was it.

A sweet and short evening. Good food, familiar faces, a little bit of socialising, and an trip to Aldi. Nothing extraordinary, but sometimes that's all you need.
