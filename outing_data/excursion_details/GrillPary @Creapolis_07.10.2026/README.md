## I am here!

I reached Creapolis at around **5:15 PM**. The party was  behind the main building where we work. There weren't that many people, and since I didn't known anyone, it felt a bit awkward for a minute. Then I saw Florian, so I went up to him and talked a bit. He told me that he wouldn't be available tomorrow, and I'm not available next week, so we'll work on something new after two weeks.


## Meeting People

After that, I met the guy who had helped me chamfer and sand then countertops two weeks ago, who then left at around **5:30 PM**. We talked for a while. I still don't know his name, but apparently, he's a computer science graduate and works for a company that builds large machines. He's responsible for taking care of the software on the installed screens. We ended up talking about the job market, internships, and a lot more.

Then Lucas joined. He came to Coburg last week and works at the Hochschule in the foundation department. They help students start businesses. There's a programme running for a year that's supposed to help us become entrepreneurs.

I spent most of my time talking to him about my past experiences, his past experiences, and more. He's just started working and wants to learn more about students and how he can help them.


## Food, Fire, and Good Vibes

We ate in between. I had a **Bratwurst with a vegan sausage, a potato, and some cheese**, all cooked on a grill. There were cold drinks too, but I chose to avoid them.

We stood near the fire for a while and talked about the weather, climate, and nothing particularly serious. That's when I saw Elly around the printing station. There was a station where you could print on almost anything you liked, which was pretty cool!

Lucas left at around **7 PM**, and I thought I'd just say hello to Elly before leaving. But then I got talking to her, and we ended up eating another Bratwurst and a potato together.

I also printed a little tote bag with **blue lemons** on it, which I absolutely loved.

I left Creapolis at around **8 PM** with Elly. We parted ways just around the corner.


##  Getting Lost

Now comes the part where my peaceful evening took a rather horrifying turn.

On my way home, I usually climb the hill after the Bahnhof, but today I decided to take a new path before that - the road you can see the buses taking. Unfortunately, the road was blocked because of construction. There were some stairs to my rigt, so, without thinking too much about it, I took them.

And that, was where things started going wrong.

At some point, I realised I was completely lost. **Even Google Maps couldn't even find me.** I was somehow in what felt like a jungle, and it was getting really dark.

At one point , I came across two paths and decided to try one of them. But it was sodark that I got spooked and turned back, thinking I'd just take the stairs down instead. Except the stairs led to a **dead end**. I was standing there, lost in the dark, with absolutely no idea where I was or how to get out. Behind me was a house. Suddenly, its motion-sensor patio light switched on, illuminating what looked like an abandoned house. Just when I was trying to process that, I noticed a small, creepy shed or possibly a toilet on the opposite side. And then **something moved**.

I did not wait around to investigate. I turned around and ran back the way I had come. At that point, I was genuinely terrified. My imagination was working overtime, and every shadow looked suspicious. I just wanted to find a normal street with some lights and other signs of human civilisation. Eventually, I found a street that was actually lit. Only then did my heartbeat slowly start to return to normal. I don't think I have ever been so happy to see streetlights.

**I am officially not trying any new paths for the foreseeable future.** If Google Maps doesn't that path, I don't want to know either.

I finally reached home at around **8:40 PM**, very happy to be indoors, safe, and far away from mysterious movements in the dark.
