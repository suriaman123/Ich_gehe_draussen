Today was a very different Soul Food evening because, for once, **there were a lot of people**!Franzi and Piama were there too, although neither of them recognised me. I guess I must have been a pretty forgettable face the last time we met. 😅

We had decided on **Kartoffelpuffer** last week, and Chief had already bought the potatoes for them. In the kitchen, it was me, Chief, and one other guy. I didn't ask his name, but he definitely looked cool. He was wearing a light green T-shirt with dark green overalls and had a tattoo of a **cut-open avocado with a heart-shaped seed** on his left arm. He also had a septum ring and a vertical bar piercing on the upper part of one of his ears.

So, while Chief prepared the onions, the other guy and I got to work on the potatoes. We peeled and shredded about **3 kg of potatoes**.mThen we mixed everything together with a lot of flour, some salt, and muskatnuss gemahlen.


## Making Kartoffelpuffer

We filled two pans with a *lot* of oil, which the other guy was not particularly happy about. 
Chief then showed me a really useful trick for checking whether the oil is hot enough. You just put a **wooden spoon vertically into the oil**, and if bubbles form around it, the oil is ready.

Once the oil was hot enough, we started putting small portions of the potato mixture into both pans. Unfortunately, one of the pans wasn't cooperating. The batter kept sticking to it, so Chief eventually gave up, emptied the pan, and **threw it in the trash**.

Problem solved.

We continued with the other pan and made a surprisingly large number of Kartoffelpuffer.

Traditionally, Kartoffelpuffer are eaten with **applesauce**. I, however, opened the refrigerator, found the ketchup, and decided that ketchup was the obvious choice. The look Chief gave me was... not encouraging. I also received several side-eyes from everyone else.
But I stand by my decision.

There were **six of us at the table**, and by the end, we had eaten all the Kartoffelpuffer. They were actually really good, and honestly, I thought they went very well with ketchup.

I still don't like applesauce.

So, ketchup wins.
