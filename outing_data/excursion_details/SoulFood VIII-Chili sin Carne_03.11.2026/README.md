# Chili sin Carne
*An evening of tofu, questionable shopping habits, and making friends.*

**Soulfood | 16:10–20:30**
<br/> 
Recipes come with a story. This one came with a shopping expedition, an illegal rave anecdote, a surprisingly good conversation, and a slightly damaged apricot dessert.


## Before we start: The shopping expedition

I arrived at Cosmos around 16:10, as usual. There were quite a few people around, which made me feel a little awkward. Max was at the counter talking to someone, while Anna and Pia were busy in the background. I stood near the counter for about ten minutes, not really knowing what to do. Eventually, things got quieter, and I found a chair in the side room.

Pia soon came over and told me we'd be making **Chili sin Carne** today. Anna and she had already prepared the shopping list and were planning to head to the supermarket once Anna was free. I volunteered to join them.

For once, I actually got to have a proper conversation with Pia. She often tries to start conversations with me, but my conversational skills usually manage to kill them within a minute. Today went much better!

By the time we were ready to leave, Tetzi had arrived and joined the shopping trip instead.

Our first stop was Netto. On the way, we talked about Halloween, my headache-induced day of sleeping, and their costumes. Anna had dressed up as a sad clown (I would really like to see a picture), while Tetzi had gone as Johnny Depp's scissors-for-hands character from *Edward Scissorhands*.
Tetzi also casually mentioned that he'd attended an illegal rave party and stayed until 5 a.m. Apparently, last year he'd attended another one in an abandoned warehouse and had even been stopped by the police for a urine drug test on the way there. Some people really do have interesting weekends.

At Netto, Tetzi spent the entire time joking about stealing things, while Anna kept trying to stop him. It was basically a running comedy show, with Anna playing the responsible adult and Tetzi doing his best to get them both into trouble. Max had given Anna just €20 for the shopping. We managed to get lettuce, a gurken, and dark chocolate from Netto before heading to Tegu for tofu and avocados.nI also discovered that Tegu has quite a few tofu varieties. Definitely something I'll have to investigate another day.At netto the entire time tezti was joking about stelling stuff and anna was try to stop him.

On the way back, we somehow got onto the subject of shoplifting stories. Anna admitted that she'd once stolen an anime figurine worth €60–70 when she was 14. She simply walked out of the shop because she looked so innocent that nobody suspected her.

Honestly, I think she could still pull it off today.

We returned to Cosmos around 17:30.

## The recipe: Chili sin Carne

*Chili con Carne traditionally contains meat. Today, we're making its meat-free cousin, Chili sin Carne, using tofu instead.*

### Ingredients

**For the chili**
- 2 packs of tofu
- 3 cartons of tomato purée (*passierte Tomaten*)
- 800 g kidney beans
- 2 cans of sweetcorn
- Around 50 g tomato paste
- 1 onion
- Garlic
- A little water
- Around 40 g dark chocolate
- Potatoes, boiled separately

**Spices**
We used a generous combination of:
- Paprika powder
- Sweet paprika
- Spicy paprika
- Garlic powder
- Black pepper
- Salt
- Cinnamon
- Cumin
- Turmeric
- Caraway (*Kümmel*)

And apparently, even more paprika. You can never have too much paprika at Soulfood.


**For the sides**
- Lettuce
- Avocados
- Yogurt
- Lemon juice
- Butter pepper (*bunter Pfeffer*)
- Baguette and white bread


### Preparation
**1. Prepare the vegetables** <br/>
I chopped the onion and garlic. Pia cut the potatoes, leaving the skin on, and boiled them in salted water for around 15 minutes. Tetzi mashed the avocados, while Anna washed and separated the lettuce leaves.

**2. Start the chili**<br/>
Pia took charge of almost all the actual cooking (I'd say 99% of it!).
She heated some oil in a large pot, added the onion and garlic, and then mixed in around 50 g of tomato paste.

After that came the main ingredients:
- Two packs of tofu
- Three cartons of tomato purée
- 800 g kidney beans
- Two cans of sweetcorn
- A little water

**3. Add the spices**

This was where things got interesting. Pia added paprika, sweet paprika, spicy paprika, garlic powder, black pepper, salt, cinnamon, cumin, turmeric, and Kümmel.
There were so many different spices that I took pictures of most of them. I'll attach those as a reference.
I asked Pia about Kümmel, but neither of us could quite settle on an English explanation. Some ingredients simply refuse to be translated.

**4. The secret ingredient**

Next came around 40 g of dark chocolate. Apparently, chocolate helps improve the consistency and balances out the acidity of the tomatoes. I wouldn't have thought of putting chocolate in chili, but it worked!

Finally, we added the boiled potatoes, gave everything a good stir, and the chili was ready.


**5. Prepare the sides**

Tetzi prepared a kind of guacamole using mashed avocado, yogurt, lemon juice, and bunter Pfeffer. There may have been a few other ingredients too, but I wasn't paying attention at that point.

I was already thinking that some bread or pasta would go really well with the chili. Luckily, Anna and Pia had the same idea and sent Tetzi out to get bread. He returned about 15 minutes later with a baguette and a white loaf. They were on an end-of-day offer, so he managed to get two for the price of one. A very successful shopping trip, I'd say.


### Serving

While Tetzi sliced the bread, I set the table. Three more people joined us, including Dino, and finally we could eat.
The chili was delicious! I even went back for a second serving of salad.

## After dinner: A small German reminder

While sitting at the table, I realised something: I really need to learn German.

Aditional people always join us, and everyone naturally switched to German. I am always the only person at the table who couldn't understand a single thing. Once I'd finished eating, I honestly didn't see much point in sitting there while everyone else continued chatting.

I cleared some things into the kitchen and got ready to leave.

Before heading out, I had a little conversation with Max. We both agreed that Soulfood evenings were great, and then I said goodbye to everyone.

I left around 19:18.

But apparently, the evening wasn't over.


## Bonus round: Lidl and Kinder Joy

I was supposed to go straight home, but instead I walked towards Lidl. I arrived at 19:42, having practically speed-walked the entire way.

The mission was simple: buy some Kinder Joys because they were on sale.

I didn't take a shopping cart, thinking I'd only pick up a few things. Naturally, my hands were full within minutes, so I started putting everything into my backpack. Everyone does this here, but it still feels a little strange to put groceries directly into your bag before paying.

There was a queue at the regular checkout, so I went to the self-checkout. One of the puddings caused an issue, and a staff member came over to help. While packing everything into my backpack, I accidentally damaged an apricot dessert cup. I just stood there awkwardly, wondering whether I should eat it, throw it away, or pretend nothing had happened.

Then I spotted the same staff member who'd helped me earlier. I waved him over and showed him the damaged cup. He asked me something that I somehow understood as, "Have you already paid for it?"

I said yes, and he simply brought me a replacement!

I thanked him with a and apologised for the little bit of cream I'd managed to leave on the self-checkout counter. A successful supermarket mission, despite the minor dessert incident.

I got home before 20:30, with more than 10,000 steps behind me.

Today was genuinely fun.

Everyone talked to me, I had proper conversations with people, and for once, it felt like we were slowly becoming friends rather than just people who happen to cook in the same kitchen.

The chili was delicious, surprisingly easy to make, and definitely something I'd like to try again.

And I got Kinder Joys.
