# Soul Food — Day 2

Location: 50.25440323060424, 10.96360184754839

I was actually quite excited about Soul Food this week. After last time, when we somehow turned zucchini and chickpeas into a really good meal, I was curious to see what we would come up with this time. I definitely would never have thought of combining those ingredients myself.

I reached Cosmos at around **4:15 PM**, but once again, I was the only participant. Piama wasn't coming either. The nice lady from last time was there; for now, let's call her **Chief**. Chief asked me what I wanted to cook, but I told her I was happy with whatever she preferred. We looked at what we already had, and there was some **pasta and pesto sauce**. At one point, I even offered to go home because I felt like Chief was only cooking because I had shown up. But she said it would be nice to have a meal together and have a conversation, so I stayed. We decided to keep things simple and make **pesto pasta** instead of coming up with a dish and going to Aldi to buy ingredients.


## Apparently, This Is How You Make Pesto Pasta

I already knew about pesto pasta because Utkarsh made it once last month. At the time, I thought he was just being lazy. Turns out, **that's actually how you make it.** There were two other guys and Ali there as well, and Chief asked them if they wanted to eat with us. They agreed. One of the guys is in a band, and he told me that pesto pasta is a pretty typical German student dish. 

We boiled about **one and a half packets of pasta** and set the table with red and green pesto, along with some vegan cheese.
And... that was basically it.
You take some boiled pasta, add a little pesto, mix it, and eat.
I didn't really like the green pesto, but the red one was okay. Still, it felt strange to me to eat pasta with basically **no vegetables at all**. It also felt like a slightly unhealthy meal if that was all you were eating. Ali pointed out, though, that I should consider the fact that a lot of students are broke and can't always afford fresh vegetables. When she put it that way, the dish started making a little more sense.

Maybe pesto pasta isn't trying to be fancy. Maybe it's just **cheap, easy student survival food**.


## Planning Next Week

After we finished eating, I helped clean up. I had completely forgotten to do that last week, which I'm still a little embarrassed about. 😅 Chief said that next week we could decide on a dish beforehand, and if she had the time, she could buy the ingredients before I arrived. I immediately suggested **pancakes**, because I still really want to learn how to make them. After talking with Ali, though, we decided on something a little different: **Kartoffelpuffer**, also called *Ditter* in Bavaria. They're usually translated as **potato pancakes**, but from what I understand, they're basically crispy potato patties. 

At least, that's my current understanding. I'll find out next week.

It was fun again, even though it was another very small group.
