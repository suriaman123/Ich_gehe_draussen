#### Location: Schlachthofstraße 1, 96450 Coburg

When I reached Creapolis, Florian was busy giving a tour. Apparently, his colleague was sick, so he had to take over the tour for the day. He pointed me towards **Kevin** and told me Kevin would help me until Florian was free.

## Meeting Kevin

A little bit about Kevin: he's Canadian, but he moved to Germany when he was seven, so he speaks really good German. He did his bachelor's degree at **Hochschule Coburg** and had been working at Creapolis for the past three years. He has now started his master's at **SRH**, which means he's no longer considered a Hochschule Coburg student and therefore cannot work at Creapolis anymore. He happened to be in the neighbourhood that day, though, so he came by to help out until around 6 PM.

Our first task was not particularly exciting. We had to remove the **protective film from all the cabinets**. It was incredibly annoying and boring, and it took us around **an hour and a half** to get through all of them. Definitely not the most glamorous part of building furniture, but someone had to do it.


## Finally Attaching the Countertops

Around **5:30 PM**, Steven and Florian joined us, and we finally got to the part we'd been working towards for the past few weeks: **attaching the countertops**.

There wasn't anything particularly complicated about it. We just positioned the countertops and drilled the screws directly into place. And just like that, the mobile workstation was basically done.

Once we had attached everything and cleaned them up a bit, we pushed them  into the kitchen to see what it actually looked like in its final position. It was pretty satisfying to finally see it there after all the cutting, sanding, varnishing, measuring, and various small problems we'd had along the way.

The three of us — Florian, Steven, and me — stayed in the kitchen for a while, rearranging things and talking about what we should work on next. Steven was ready to start another project immediately. Florian and I, on the other hand, were pretty tired.

Florian said he wanted to add **one more coat of varnish**, so I stayed behind for a little while longer while Steven left around **7 PM**. I eventually left around **7:30 PM**, after promising Florian that I would **definitely register for next week's grill party**.


## Three Days, Ten Hours, One Counter

It's actually kind of funny when I think about it.

It took us roughly **three days and around 10 hours** of work just to change the countertops and attach the legs and wheels to a store-bought cabinet.

A lot of sanding.

A lot of measuring.

A lot of varnish.

And eventually, two  mobile workstation.

It was tiring, but honestly, it was also a lot of fun. I learned quite a few new things and got to work with some interesting people along the way.


## What's Next?

We're not completely sure what we'll be working on next week, but there are already two possible projects.

The first is to install a **suction system above the resin 3D printers** to remove the potentially hazardous fumes produced while printing.

The second is to **lower the sitting area** that we previously decided was designed by evil architects. 😄

We'll see what happens next week.

For now, the mobile workstation is finally done.
