## Game Time!

Once dinner was over, we moved on to the games. First up was Susane's usual game, which is essentially bingo with a social twist.

Everyone gets a sheet containing a list of statements. You have to go around asking different people whether each statement applies to them. If someone agrees, you can write their name down next to that statement. The catch is that you can ask only one statement per person, so you can't simply get all your answers from the same friendly stranger.

To win bingo, you need four names in a row, either horizontally or diagonally.

I went around asking people questions, and gradually filled in my sheet. Afther the German girl and Prince I was the thrid to say Bingo!, and we had to stand on a chair to declare our achievement. It's a pretty good icebreaker because it gives you an actual reason to approach people rather than having to figure out how to start a conversation from scratch. That part, at least, works in my favour!

After bingo, we played another game that turned out to be even more fun.


### The GAME!!!!

The game involved everyone standing in a circle, with one person in the middle acting as the conductor. The person in the middle points at someone and calls out a predetermined word. The person pointed at and the two people standing next to them must immediately perform their assigned actions. Anyone who forgets their action or gets it wrong has to swap places with the person in the middle.

We started with three words and added two more later. The five words were:

- **Elephant:** The person in the middle has to make a trunk with their arm, while the people on either side make the elephant's ears.

Simple enough, until someone points at you unexpectedly and your brain decides to stop working.

- **James Bond:** The person in the middle strikes a cool James Bond pose with finger guns, while the people on either side pretend to hold the middle person's arms and say, "Oh, James!"

This one got a lot of laughs. 

- **Washing Machine:** The two people on either side form the walls of a washing machine using their arms, while the person in the middle spins around.

- **Lantern:** The person in the middle stands tall with one hand raised in the air, while the people on either side lift one leg and pretend to pee.

Yes, really. If Markus came up with these actions on his own, he has some crazy inguinity.

- **Crocodile:** The person in the middle makes crocodile jaws with their hands, while the people on either side make a loud *grawww* noise.

By this point, everyone was trying to react as quickly as possible, and the whole thing was wonderfully chaotic.


The best part was that you had to remember your own action while also keeping track of what everyone around you was supposed to do. It was the sort of game where you could know the rules perfectly well and still mess up the moment someone pointed at you.

We had a genuinely good time playing it.

After that, we played a single round of charades before the tour guide arrived. I asked Markus what the second game was called, but he didn't know either. So, unfortunately, I still don't have a name to search for if I want to play it again!
