# Creapolis Community Area Makeover
Location: Schlachthofstraße 1, 96450 Coburg

## 10.09.2026


I left home around 3:30 PM, hoping to reach Creapolis by 4:00. Somehow, I managed to get lost and ended up walking for an extra 40 minutes before finally making it there. Not exactly the smoothest start to the day.

I went to Creapolis to help with a **Community Area makeover**. The main project for the day was working on some kitchen cabinets and trying to build a bigger countertop for them.

We started by figuring out where the screws needed to go. I measured the screw spacing on the cabinet, and Florian made a template with four screw positions. I then used the template to mark and drill the holes into the wooden supports.
After that, we moved on to the countertops. We cut two pieces of wood and I worked on **chamfering and sanding** both pieces. It was actually pretty satisfying seeing the rough pieces of wood slowly start to look like something that could eventually become a proper countertop.

Unfortunately, that's when we ran into a problem.We realised that the two wooden pieces weren't exactly the same size, so we had to cut them again. After chamfering them for the second time, though, there was still a pretty huge difference in their sizes. At that point, we decided it was better to stop rather than keep trying to force it to work.

Florian is going to fix the wood-cutting machine, and I'll meet him again the **Thursday after next** to hopefully continue the project.

One of the nice surprises of the day was getting a **free drink and a meal**. A really nice man (sorry i forgot his name) made sour eggs and boiled eggs, which was a pretty unique taste for me. I'm not sure if I would ever  order it myself, but I'm glad I got to try it!

Florian won't be there next week, but I think I might still go to Creapolis next Thursday — maybe just for the meal. 😂

By the time I finally got home at around **9:30 PM**, I had a major headache. I barely made it into my room before going straight to sleep.

So, overall: a long walk, some woodworking, a slightly problematic countertop, free food, and a massive headache.
Not a bad day, all things considered.
