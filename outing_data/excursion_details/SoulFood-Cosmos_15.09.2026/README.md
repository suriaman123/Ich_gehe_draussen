# SoulFood at Cosmos

**15.09.2026**

Last Saturday, Ali told me about something called **SoulFood**. The idea is pretty simple: people come together, discuss what they want to cook, go buy the ingredients, and then cook and eat together.The week before, they had made pancakes, which caught my attention because I've been wanting to try making pancakes myself for quite a while. So when I heard about SoulFood, I really wanted to go.

There was just one problem.I had a headache since the morning.

I came back from Sonneberg around **2 PM**, and I was still debating whether I should go or just stay home. After having a pretty heavy lunch, I somehow convinced myself to go anyway. I reached Cosmos at around **4 PM**. There was no one there except me, so we waited for about 20 minutes. When it seemed like nobody else was going to show up, we decided to just start cooking with whoever was there.

And, by the way, Ali was there too, but she didn't recognise me! I tried saying hello twice, but she seemed to ignore me, so I decided not to push it. 😅

In the end, it was me, a nice lady, and Piama who started cooking together.


## Cooking With What We Had

There were some **boiled chickpeas left over from Saturday**, so we decided to make something with them. We started by heating some oil in a pan and added **tomato paste, sugar, diced zucchini, and soy sauce**. We sautéed everything for a while before adding the boiled chickpeas.

Then came the best part: **a lot of spices**. We also added a little cornflour to thicken the broth ( quoting Piama here). While we were cooking the chickpeas, the nice lady prepared some **salad and rice** on the side.

Eventually, the guy who had been there on Saturday night also arrived, so the four of us sat down together to eat.

The food was actually really good. I tried the salad too, although there were honestly a lot of leaves in it for my liking. 😅 The guy mostly ate the salad, though, and he seemed to be enjoying it, so maybe I just need to improve my relationship with leafy vegetables. Everyone was talking in German while we were eating, so I didn't understand **a single thing** that was being said. But honestly, I was perfectly happy just sitting there, eating and enjoying the food.

And with how tasty the food turned out, I  felt like I would have also **raided India for it**.

The nice lady gave me the leftovers to take home, and I was more than happy to accept them. Free food that I don't have to cook myself? Absolutely.

All in all, it was a really fun little outing. I got to cook with people, try something new, eat good food, and bring some leftovers home.

I reached home around **7:15 PM**.

And yes...

**I still had a headache.** 😅
