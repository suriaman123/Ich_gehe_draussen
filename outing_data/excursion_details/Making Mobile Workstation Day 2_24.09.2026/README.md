# Creapolis Mobile Workstation — Day 2
Location: Schlachthofstraße 1, 96450 Coburg
<!-- 24.09.2026 -->

I left home around **3:45 PM**. The next bus was in about 10 minutes, so instead of waiting, I decided to walk. And, of course, I almost took the wrong road again. Eventually, I made it to Creapolis, where Florian and two other volunteers were already there. Florian had already cut the countertops down to roughly the same size, so our job for the day was to get them properly finished. One of the volunteers and I worked on **chamfering and sanding the countertops**, while the other volunteer was sanding the wooden legs.

By the way, I finally found out that the nice guy I've been calling **“Chief”** actual name. His name is **Peter**. Peter had apparently told Florian that he was going to take an **English-language introductory course for 3D printing and the laser cutter**. So it looks like I'll get to use the 3D printers at Creapolis soon.

## Windbeutel and Evil Architects

Around **5 PM**, we took a break. Florian brought out some frozen **Windbeutel** — pastries filled with vanilla cream. They were actually really fun to eat, and definitely made for a good break from sanding wood. While eating, we somehow got onto the topic of how **evil architects are**. The whole sitting area at Creapolis looks really nice and aesthetic, but apparently isn't particularly comfortable. Florian joked that maybe, for the next project, he might just redesign the entire sitting area himself. We also talked about Creapolis itself. Apparently, its current funding is expected to end in about **a year and a half**, and nobody really knows what will happen after that.

Another funny fact I learned is that the thing we're currently building officially has to be called a **“mobile workstation.”**
Why?... Because apparently, they can't justify spending money on **mobile kitchen counters**. So, a mobile kitchen counter became a mobile workstation. Problem solved.


## Back to Work

After the break, we went back to the countertops. We **watered and sanded them**, getting them ready for varnishing. By around **5:30 PM**, the volunteer working with me had to leave. I completely forgot to ask his name before he left, so he remains a mystery for now.

Through out the day I kept getting the strange feeling that it was raining outside. Every once in a while, I would hear something that sounded like rain and look out the window, only to realise that it wasn't raining at all. Apparently, there is a **small river behind the building**, and the sound of the water was what I was hearing. I hadn't noticed it before, but once I knew it was there, I realised how pretty the view actually was. It was a surprisingly nice little detail to discover while working.
 
I then applied a coat of varnish to the countertops. After letting it sit for a while, I gave them a very light sanding and then added another, thicker coat of varnish.

Oh, my headache had actually started while I was still at home getting ready to leave. I’m not sure exactly when, but while I was working near the open window, the cool air seemed to do the trick, and luckly my headache was gone. Happy for the windfall!


## A Little India Discussion

During another break, the conversation somehow turned towards **India**. Florian's wife studied in India, and they had recently been invited to a wedding there. Florian had also travelled to India and had quite a few stories to tell.

He mentioned how strange he found the traffic and driving culture. At one point, he described being caught in traffic while being on foot and also mentioned seeing a tailor's shop that was smaller than the piece of wood we were currently working with.
But the most surprising story was about a **brick factory** he had seen, either in Agra or Kolkata — I am not completely sure where.Apparently, the factory was using a very old furnace where the heat and cooling process worked in a circular motion. New bricks had to constantly be added while old ones were removed. According to him, the process was dangerous for the people working there and produced a lot of pollution. He summed up his frustration with a line that stuck with me:
> “It's not like India can't use cleaner air.”

Our conversation went on for longer than expected, which made our break longer than planned. Eventually, though, we remembered that we were supposed to be building something and got back to work.


## Almost Finished

We attached all the **legs and wheels to the bottom of the countertops**, which made the whole thing finally start looking like an actual mobile workstation.By the time we finished, it was around **8 PM**. While we were attaching the legs, I remembered to ask the other volunteer his name. His name is **Steven**. The last train to Bamberg was coming up soon, so we started cleaning everything up. Steven and I left around **8:20 PM**, while Florian stayed behind to close the place.

I walked home slowly and reached just before **9 PM**. We're almost done now. Next time, we just need to attach the actual countertops, and the mobile workstation should be finished.

Oh, and apparently there's a **grill party on the 7th** at Creapolis. I haven't looked into it properly yet, but I'm already looking forward to it.
