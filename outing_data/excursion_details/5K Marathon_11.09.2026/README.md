# 5K FirmenLauf

**11.09.2026**

The **FirmenLauf** `https://www.neue-presse-firmenlauf.de` is an annual run organised by Neue Presse. Around **2,000 people** showed up this year, and it was less like a serious running event and more like a huge community gathering. People from all over Coburg came together to run, walk, eat, dance, and just have a good time.

I went with **Utkarsh and Prince**, who actually ran the entire 5K and finished in around **35 minutes**. I, on the other hand, decided that walking was a much more realistic goal for me. It took me about **an hour**, but hey — I finished!

I wore a red **Hochschule Coburg T-shirt with my name on it**, which made me feel a little more like I was part of the event.

Honestly, I didn't think I would be able to complete 5 kilometres. I was expecting to struggle, but somehow I made it all the way to the finish line. It was actually a pretty great feeling.

What made the whole thing even more fun was seeing just how many different people were taking part. There were **five-year-old kids, older people, parents pushing children in trams, people accompanying relatives in wheelchairs, firefighters running in full uniform and equipment**, and even a **three-legged dog**!

It really didn't feel like everyone was there to compete. Everyone was just taking part in their own way.

The people living along the route were also cheering us on from their **balconies and lawns**, which made the walk so much more enjoyable. There was cheering, music, people laughing, and just a really nice atmosphere everywhere.

By the end of the day, I had walked around **23,000 steps**.

For someone who wasn't even sure they could finish 5K, I'm pretty happy with that.

**5 kilometres: completed. ✅**  
**23,000 steps: somehow also completed. 😅**
