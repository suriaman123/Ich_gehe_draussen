# A Tour of Creapolis
Location: Schlachthofstraße 1, 96450 Coburg

**17.09.2026**

I reached Creapolis just before **6 PM**. As Florian had told me the week before, he wasn't there today.I also tried looking for the nice guy who had made the sour eggs the previous week, but he wasn't there either. I sat outside for a while, mostly on my phone, and was just about to leave when I saw him arriving on a bicycle.

He apologised and told me that there wouldn't be any food that day, and I told him not to worry because I had come anyway just to say hello to him. We went inside, and after talking for a bit, he asked me if I had ever had a proper tour of Creapolis. I said no, so after taking a little breather, he decided to show me around.


## The Creapolis Tour

We started with the **metalworking machines**, then moved on to the woodworking area. From there, he showed me the **3D printers, laser cutter, embroidery machine**, and a bunch of other equipment.

There was a particular person who  had been printed and displayed quite a lot around the place, so I asked about him. That question somehow turned into a much bigger history lesson. He ended up telling me a lot about **Coburg's history**, including stories about **Duke Ernest, Prince Albert and how he became a prince, and Queen Victoria**. I wasn't expecting to get a history lesson when I walked into Creapolis, but I definitely wasn't complaining.

At the end of the tour, he told me that he would give me an **introduction course on 3D printers** the Thursday after next. Apparently, you need to complete the introduction before you can book and use one of the 3D printers.

And that's something I really want to do, so I'm looking forward to it.


There was, however, one small problem. I still don't know this guy's name. We've met twice now, he's given me food, shown me around Creapolis, and told me stories about Coburg — and somehow I never asked his name. At this point, I'm honestly too scared to ask. So, until I figure it out, I have decided to call him **Chief**.

Chief also told me something interesting about Florian. Apparently, Florian is something of a big deal when it comes to **wood and laser-cutting technology**. He apparently developed a way of making boxes that is now being used across Europe. I definitely need to ask Florian about this next time I see him.

I reached home around **8:15–8:30 PM**. There was no free meal this time, but I still had a really good time. The only downside was that my headache started around **6:15 PM** and stayed with me for the rest of the evening.

No food, but a tour, a history lesson, and a future 3D-printing course?

I'd still call that a pretty good visit.
