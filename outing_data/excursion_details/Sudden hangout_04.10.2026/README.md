## Meeting Khaya

Khaya was in Coburg and came by to collect some stuff I had brought from India for Pinto, since she’s currently living near Stuttgart. I went downstairs, gave her the stuff, and we chatted for a bit about how life was going, how her internship was going, and how I was feeling pretty exhausted lately.

Shadat also happened to pass by. Apparently, he and Khaya were friends. But honestly, the questions he asked her felt a little weird to me. Khaya mentioned that she had started her internship in September, and he immediately started asking whether she had got her master’s thesis, whether she had talked to them about it, and what she planned to do for a full-time job after her thesis. I mean, I get that these things are important, but that was a lot of questions to throw at someone who had just  started an internship!

Shadat himself is doing his master’s thesis at ISAT, and his topic is related to bone reconstruction. I have absolutely no idea how that works, but it sounds pretty interesting and important.


## An Unexpected Trip to the City Centre

After a while, Prince showed up, and he and Khaya planned to go to the city centre. Prince invited me along. I was a little reluctant at first, but eventually agreed to join them. I went upstairs to grab my jacket and put on my shoes, and off we went.

Since it was Sunday, there were no buses running, so we had to walk. We met Duc at the Bahnhof, and he decided to join us too. I was happy about that because now I had someone to talk to while we walked. Before Duc joined us, I was feeling a little left out. The pavements are not big enough for three people to walk in a line, and I was struggling to hear  their conversations. It was a relief to have Duc around!


## Ice Cream, Coffee, and More Talking

We eventually reached **Cortina**, where the others decided to get ice cream. I had a sore throat, so I decided to skip the ice cream and eat an energy bar I had in my jacket pocket instead. The other three enjoyed their ice creams while we talked about our experiences in **China and Italy** last semester.

After a while, Khaya started craving coffee, so we headed to **Café 10**. Now, I don't drink coffee, but Prince was like, “At least order something!” So I ordered water. 😂 Except the water they brought was ice-cold and came with a glass filled with  ice. Not exactly what I wanted with a sore throat! So I got it exchanged for an empty glass and a bottle of room-temperature water. Much better.

We continued talking about our time abroad. China felt more like a vacation, while Italy was basically backbreaking work. It was fun comparing our experiences and hearing what everyone thought about the previous semester.

After spending quite a while talking, we finally headed back. Khaya went to meet her friend, while we boys made our way back to **Hochschule**.


## A Nice Sunday

All in all, it was a fun day of catching up with everyone. Nothing particularly exciting happened—we just walked around, talked about our lives, and compared our experiences abroad.
Sometimes, that's all you need for a nice Sunday!
