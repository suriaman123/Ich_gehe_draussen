## Making Pumpkin Soup

Chef had already bought all the ingredients for pumpkin soup, so we got started.

I peeled the potatoes and carrots, Max chopped the pumpkin, and Anna was supposed to cut the onions. Unfortunately, she started tearing up just from peeling them, so I took over the onion-cutting duties.

By the way, Anna was super fun! She's young, probably around 16 and definitely not older than 18, and she's quite dramatic in the funniest way. Even when everyone was speaking German and I couldn't understand much of the conversation, I could still figure out what was happening from her gasps and expressions. 😂

At some point, Elly came into the kitchen and asked if I'd ever tried a particular Bavarian food. I can't remember its name, but it's basically a pretzel-based, french-cheese-and-butter kind of thing. The English translation was something like *pull-apart* or *tear-apart*. Since I'd never tried it before, she gave me some to taste. Apparently, it's quite popular in Bavaria. Well, now I've tried it, and I have to say it would taste better if eaten warm; it would be much better with a cup of tea or coffee.

Anyway, back to the soup!

We put the ingredients into a pot with some coconut oil. Meanwhile, Max roasted some pumpkin seeds in a separate pan and then caramelised a few slices of pumpkin to use as garnish.
It wasn't a particularly complicated dish, but it was definitely time-consuming. At some point, we also added a can of coconut milk. I still don't know exactly what *Gemüsebrühe* is, but apparently, we needed it, so Anna went out to buy some.

Once she came back, we blended everything in the pot, added the *Gemüsebrühe*, salt, and black pepper, and that was pretty much it. Pumpkin soup was ready!

Simple enough, but somehow, the whole process took a lot out of me. I don't know what it was, but I got unusually tired today. This was actually the first time I'd stayed at Cosmos past 6 PM, so maybe that had something to do with it.


## Dinner with Everyone

Once the soup was ready, Max and I set the table and called everyone over. By this time, Tenzi had arrived too, and he cut up some pumpkin seed bread to go with the soup. Two more guys joined us, and we all sat down to eat together.

It was a nice meal, and I enjoyed having everyone around. These occasional group dinners are lovely.

After dinner, I helped clean up the table and left around **7:15–7:30 PM**.

## My Favourite Soul Food Day So Far

All in all, today was a lot of fun. The kitchen was lively, Max and Anna had each other to talk to, and I could happily stay in my little corner, help with the cooking, and enjoy the atmosphere.

People-wise, **this was my favourite Soul Food day so far**.

I know I won't understand every conversation when everyone speaks German, and I know not every Tuesday will be this fun. But today felt comfortable. I got to cook something new, meet people, and spend a few hours in a place where I genuinely enjoy being.

I hope I get to have more Tuesdays like this, at least for as long as I'm in Germany.
