Last Tuesday, I met Utkarsh at Cosmos. He was talking to Pia and happened to mention that his birthday was on 14 November. I made a mental note of it because I wanted to wish him exactly at midnight. Initially, I planned to get him the ice-cream pastry he likes from Kaufland. However, since I had spent most of the week lazing around, I ended up going with a different option. I picked up a really nice-looking pudding instead. Well, presentation matters too!

Since the Go! program, my sleep schedule had been all over the place. I had been randomly falling asleep at different times throughout the day. So today, when I woke up from my second nap at around 20:00, I decided to set an alarm for 23:50.

Thankfully, I had the sense to do that because I fell asleep again!. The alarm eventually woke me up. I washed my face, picked up my aesthetic pudding and headed downstairs at 23:55. As soon as I reached the first floor, I spotted Sagar carrying an ice cake. Apparently, I wasn't the only one who had planned a midnight surprise! Soon, Manish and Naveen bhai joined us, and the four of us headed to Utkarsh's room. Unmesh was already there. A minute later, Archit and two more people arrived.

And then it was midnight!

We all wished Utkarsh a very happy birthday and cut the cake. Archit and the other two people left shortly afterwards, while the rest of us stayed back and continued chatting. There were quite a few fun conversations about life, my questionable sleep schedule, FMS guys going to the gym and several other random topics. Basically, the usual late-night discussions.

Soon, Archit and the other two returned and one of them ate the pudding I had brought. I hope atleast he appreciated contribution to the birthday celebration.

Then a lady came out of the adjacent room, attracted by all the noise we were making. She lived right next door, so I guess we had managed to disturb the neighbours a little.

But that didn't stop us.

We decided to celebrate Utkarsh's birthday again! We dimmed the lights, lit the candle, sang Happy Birthday and cut the leftover cake. A minute later, another guy arrived, so naturally, we repeated the entire celebration all over again.

At this point, Utkarsh had probably received more birthday wishes and cake-cutting ceremonies in one night than most people get in a year. Eventually, everyone started leaving. I believe the others went to Archit's room, while I headed back to mine.

I reached my room at around 00:40.

Now, a small thing about this whole event: I have never wished anyone happy birthday in person exactly at midnight. Nor have I ever put this much effort into making sure someone gets a birthday wish right at 12am.

But there's a first time for everything, and I'm glad it was Utkarsh's birthday.
